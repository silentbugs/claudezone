# Sound effect credits

All files in this folder are real recordings (or edits/mixes of real recordings) released under **CC0 1.0 / public domain**. No attribution is legally required; credit is given here as a courtesy and for provenance. No Call of Duty / Activision or other game-ripped audio is used. Several Freesound uploads by *qubodup* are extracts from US Department of Defense / NATO video footage (public domain as works of the US federal government) that the uploader released as CC0.

Files were trimmed, mixed to mono and peak-normalized; one-shots re-encoded as 128 kbps MP3, loops stored as 16-bit mono WAV with a baked-in crossfade. See `manifest.json` for per-file cut points and notes.
